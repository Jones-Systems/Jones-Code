# Workstreams

Use Workstreams to organize active conversations by an objective across repositories and connected environments. Repository projects still select where a conversation runs.

Choose **New Workstream** in the sidebar toolbar, enter a name, then choose **Create**. Escape or clicking outside dismisses the entry. Right-click a thread and open **Assign thread** below **Snooze** to choose a Workstream, or drag its row into a group. Choose **Unassigned (remove primary)** in the submenu to remove its primary membership; secondary associations remain and are labeled in the submenu. Existing secondary associations and membership history remain available when you open the group details.

Collapse a group to save space; your choice is remembered in this browser. Drag a group's six-dot grip to reorder it, or use **Move up** and **Move down**. Within a group, drag a thread onto another thread to place it before or after that conversation. Thread ordering uses the connected environment's native active order.

Workstreams need a connected owner registry with current access. Groups show each thread's stored assignment, so threads stay in their groups when a short-lived verification expires, while reconnecting, and in large thread lists. Threads appear under **Unassigned** only after every stored assignment has loaded; otherwise the ungrouped threads appear under **Assignment unknown**. On web and desktop, assigning a thread re-checks its current assignment and registers and verifies its reference when needed. A thread from another connected environment is registered and assigned through that environment, so you need current write access there too. Nothing changes when that environment is disconnected, needs you to sign in again, has no Workstreams set up or only read access, cannot verify the thread, or uses a different owner registry than the one the sidebar shows. If Workstreams cannot be refreshed, the sidebar keeps the last loaded groups, shows **Showing the last loaded Workstreams. Refresh to update.**, and blocks membership changes until a refresh succeeds; losing access or signing out hides them. A thread with conflicting assignments shows a warning, and its membership cannot change until it is verified again. If verification fails or access changes, refresh before trying again. Read-only access lets you inspect groups and their history. It does not enable registry changes.

Choose **Color…** from a Workstream's menu to preview a preset or custom RGB border. **Save** shares the choice with your connected devices; **Cancel** keeps the saved color, and **Reset to automatic** restores the default after saving. On mobile, open the group's menu to choose its color. Color editing requires a registry that supports shared appearance and current write access.

Changing membership preserves the conversation's repository, project, pinning, snooze and settlement state. Pinned, snoozed and settled conversations keep their native sidebar shelves.

Each group shows running threads out of its total known primary members, including members in those shelves and members whose verification has expired. Secondary associations do not increase the total. A purple dot marks threads waiting for input or approval, followed by a blue spinner while threads are running. **Failed** replaces both indicators when a member fails; the running count remains visible.

A completed turn or settled thread does not complete a Workstream. Open its details and change **Owner-declared workstream lifecycle** to record completion. Completion is shown as verified only when its owner declaration is present in the permanent history. Owner statements add context separately. T3 settlement and restore requests are explicit, separate actions.
